🔗 NOOR Connections

Privacy first: Nothing is imported or shared automatically. Choose individual contacts and press Share to NOOR. Shared contacts become visible to signed-in NOOR members. Do not share anyone's details without permission.

📱 WhatsApp / Phone Contacts

Web browsers cannot read the private contacts stored inside the WhatsApp app. You can choose contacts from your device address book (where supported) or import a contacts vCard (.vcf) exported by you. This does not read WhatsApp chats or message history.
